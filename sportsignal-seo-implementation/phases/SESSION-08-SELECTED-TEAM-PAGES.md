# Session: Selected Club Pages and Allowlist


## Objective
Align team page generation and indexability with the selected-club model.

## Required work
- Use the manually curated, season-stable selected-club allowlist confirmed in Session 1.
- Provide/maintain a simple admin/configuration method consistent with current architecture; no heavy management system.
- Generate indexable team pages only for selected clubs.
- Audit existing non-selected team pages for GSC demand, backlinks, internal links, and duplicate aliases before noindex/redirect/404 decisions.
- Ensure selected team pages answer next-match intent with upcoming selected fixtures, competition and recent/final result information already available.
- Ensure sitemap includes selected teams only.
- Do not create dedicated pages for every team in data feeds.

## Acceptance criteria
Tests cover selected and non-selected teams; only allowed team URLs can be indexed/sitemap-listed; team names and aliases are consistent; no regressions in match details.
