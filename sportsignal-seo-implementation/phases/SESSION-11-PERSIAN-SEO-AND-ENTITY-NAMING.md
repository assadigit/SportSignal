# Session: Persian SEO and Entity Naming


## Objective
Standardize Persian copy and metadata using actual query research and product language.

## Required work
- Standardize the brand as «اسپرت سیگنال» in Persian.
- Create/verify a controlled dictionary for Persian club, player (only if displayed), competition and tournament names; preserve familiar Persian search variants without awkward machine translations.
- Prioritize natural queries like «بازی بعدی بارسلونا»، «تاریخ بازی بارسلونا و منچستر سیتی» and similar validated patterns.
- Use «لیگ برتر انگلیس»، «لالیگا»، «سری آ»، «بوندس‌لیگا»، «لیگ یک فرانسه» and «لیگ قهرمانان اروپا» consistently.
- Avoid repeating generic phrases such as «باشگاه فوتبال» where unnatural.
- Do not claim full coverage of every fixture; explain the curated match selection.

## Acceptance criteria
Persian titles/H1/descriptions are idiomatic, consistent, non-duplicative and mapped to validated intent. No keyword stuffing and no machine-translated wording.
