# Session: Keyword Research Validation and Expansion


## Objective
This is the dedicated keyword research session. Validate and expand the supplied 200-phrase seed bank before any keyword-driven code or content implementation.

## Required work
- Read `KEYWORD-RESEARCH.md`, `keyword-bank-200.csv`, GSC export and Session 1 audit.
- Re-query available GSC data (prefer up to 16 months); obtain query/page associations when available; segment by Iran/mobile/Persian and English/country separately.
- Confirm exact selected-club allowlist from repo. Do not research/target team pages for non-selected teams unless they appear under an approved match exception.
- Validate phrases with Keyword Planner if authorized, Google Trends, autocomplete, actual localized Google SERPs and related searches. Record exact country, language, date, tool and evidence. Never invent volume or difficulty.
- Analyze target SERPs to classify intent and competitor formats. Focus on differences between a full-fixture/live-score service and a curated “important matches” product.
- Expand the list to at least 200 validated or evidence-tagged candidates only if useful; keep exact GSC phrases separate from suggested variants.
- Cluster synonyms and variants into one landing-page intent; identify cannibalization risks.
- Exclude live score, live match status, TV/broadcast, betting and unsupported information intents.

## Deliverables
- `seo-implementation/keyword-research/KEYWORD-VALIDATION-REPORT.md`
- `seo-implementation/keyword-research/VALIDATED-KEYWORD-MAP.csv`
- `seo-implementation/keyword-research/SERP-SAMPLES.md`
- proposed revised priority list and evidence log.

## Acceptance criteria
At least 200 candidate phrases are documented with evidence class, language, geography, intent, short/mid/long-tail, target page type, priority and status. Search volume is only populated when supported by a named source and location/time basis. No keyword is assigned to an ineligible URL type.
