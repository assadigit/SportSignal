## 1) The diagnosis came first — LIVE, before any edit

The bar was never gone for new visitors. Verified end-to-end in a real
browser (agent-browser, 390px/360px mobile) against the live production
site BEFORE touching anything:

- The `#tgPillBox` markup, the inline CSS (fixed bottom, full-width
  grass surface, cyan glow) and the inline controller all serve on
  every page load (the HTML is network-first for navigations — no stale
  SW problem; confirmed `sw.js` strategy).
- The `.show` reveal fires on the 20% scroll gate; the bar renders at
  the viewport bottom edge (rect bottom = viewport height, full width,
  76px tall at zero safe-area); VLM screenshots confirm the visual:
  the grass photo background, the reworded Persian heading, the
  rotating sub-headline, the cyan halo, the 58px blue Telegram mark.
- Fresh visitors see it in **fa and en** (the 3.2s rotator re-reads
  `html[lang]` every tick — verified by flipping lang and watching the
  sub-headline switch to English mid-view).

What hid it on the owner's own phone: the pill's **30-day tap-silence
memory**. ANY tap on the bar body or the blue mark (checking their own
channel — of course the owner taps it!) saves `sp_pill_joined6` and
silences the bar for that browser for 30 days. A tap after v13.247
(Nov 8) put the owner's browser inside the window → "it's gone".
This is the **S32 "invisible pill" report verbatim** — same root cause,
same fix. The bar had NOT regressed through S37→S41: `git log -S`
confirmed the S40 round never touched the pill code (0 pill-touching
lines in its public.ts diff), and the S33 record already documented the
legacy `#joinSticky` bot bar as deliberately inert.

## 2) The fix — the one-shot memory re-key (the S32–S36 precedent)

`sp_pill_joined6 -> sp_pill_joined7` in the inline controller
(`src/pages/public.ts`, three spots: the comment, the `lsGet` read, the
`lsSet` write). The old key is simply never read again, so:

- every visitor who tapped since v13.247 — **the owner included** —
  sees the sticky channel bar again immediately;
- fresh taps re-seed the 30-day window under the new name (the no-nag
  law survives).

An HTML-only round: no bundle edit, no CSS rename, **no SW bump** (the
SW `CACHE` is only bumped for client-referenced asset changes; the
served HTML is network-first). Nothing else changes — the grass surface,
the cyan glow stack, the full-width geometry, the 58px absolute mark,
the five-line rotator, the 20% scroll gate and the no-dismiss law ride
byte-identical (all re-pinned in the new v13.252 live check).
