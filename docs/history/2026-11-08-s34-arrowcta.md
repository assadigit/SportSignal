## What shipped (the feature round, on main)

1. THE GROWN HEADING — the pill's fixed heading carries the owner's
   full sentence at 14px/800 wrapped to at most two lines (the
   `-webkit-line-clamp:2` box; the S33 one-line 15.5px
   nowrap/ellipsis law could never carry the longer wording — on a
   390px phone the sentence measures ~283px against a ~250px line).
   The EN mirror grows with it ("Join our channel so you never miss
   the big matches!") and BOTH spans carry explicit `dir` — the EN
   sentence's trailing "!" would otherwise bidi-jump to the left edge
   of the RTL paragraph.
2. THE NUDGING ARROW — `.tgp-arrow`, a sky-blue (#8EE3FF) curved
   arrow with a static drop-shadow glow, rides BETWEEN the pill body
   and the blue mark as a FLEX CHILD of `#tgPillBox` (the writing
   mode does the placement: FA/RTL pill → arrow → mark; EN/LTR
   mirrors via `html[dir="ltr"]` + `scaleX(-1)` on the root svg).
   The nudge animation lives on the inner `<g class="tgp-ain">` —
   the root carries the mirror and a keyframe transform on the root
   would overwrite it (the CSS-animation-overrides-transform rule);
   the g's `translateX(-5px)` rides the SVG user grid (~3.7px on
   screen) toward the mark, with a soft .7→1 opacity breathe,
   1.35s infinite. Purely decorative: aria-hidden,
   pointer-events:none; transform+opacity only (the v13.242
   animation law); killed under prefers-reduced-motion.
3. THE BIGGER MARK — `#tgPillGo` grows 46 → 58px (the circled-plane
   glyph 27 → 34px, +26%); the glow ring, brand blues, href and the
   30-day-silence tap behaviour are unchanged.
4. THE MEMORY RESET — `sp_pill_joined3` → `sp_pill_joined4` (the
   S32/S33 one-shot trick): every visitor who tapped during S33
   sees the new round once; fresh taps re-seed the 30-day window.

## Layout math (why the pieces are the sizes they are)

- 360px phone (the tightest target): box 336 = pill 228 + gap 8 +
  arrow 34 + gap 8 + mark 58. Pill inner text width 196px → the
  heading wraps to exactly 2 lines (~283px of glyph run), the
  longest sub line (~157px) fits with no ellipsis. MEASURED in the
  browser: `scrollHeight 42 == clientHeight 42` (2 × 21px lines —
  no clamp cut at either 360 or 390px, FA and EN).
- The pill body stays the fixed 74px (the v13.244 law): heading 42 +
  gap 3 + sub 18 = 63px of text, ~11px of breathing room.
