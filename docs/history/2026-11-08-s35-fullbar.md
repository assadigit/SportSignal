## Layout math (why the pieces are the sizes they are)

- The page-padding law follows the new geometry: the bar occupies
  77px (74 content + 3 border, content-box) + safe-area at the
  bottom, so `body.tg-pill-on` pads `calc(78px + safe-area)` (was
  78+12+safe) and `#backTop` lifts to `calc(90px + safe-area)` (was
  110+safe — 78px bar zone + 12px gap).
- The hidden slide shrinks to `translateY(calc(100% + 8px))` — with
  `bottom:0` a plain 100% already clears the viewport (the height
  now includes the safe-area strip), the 8px keeps a hair of
  clearance.
- Text lane at 360px (the tightest target): 360 − 16 (inline-start
  padding) − 84 (mark lane) = 260px — MORE than S34's 196px; the
  reworded sentence (~265px glyph run at 14px/800) wraps to two
  lines inside the clamp exactly as the S34 sentence did.
- The mark's vertical centring: `bottom:calc(10px + safe/2)` puts
  its centre at 39px from the bar bottom on Android (the pill's
  content centre is 38.5px — 0.5px off, imperceptible) and keeps it
  centred on the full-height bar on iPhones.
