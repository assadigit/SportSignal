## 2. THE CRITICAL FINDING — the finished-match 404 cliff (159 URLs)

**GSC evidence:** Coverage export → "Not found (404)" = **159 pages**
(critical-issues class). The performance export independently shows **161 of
264 match pages** (61%) whose kickoff is on/before Nov 8 — all of them return
404 today (live-checked: PSG–Barcelona 854 impr/35 cl, Real Madrid–RB Leipzig
388/19, Atalanta–Kairat 351/2, Santander–Real Madrid 302/5, Man Utd–Roma
283/2, Monaco–Nordsjælland 275/1, Lens–Como 234/0, Barcelona–Aston Villa
175/17 — every one a 404 right now). Those 161 pages carry **5,072
impressions (52% of all match-page impressions) and 131 clicks** of this
window's history.

**Root cause (verified in source + live):** the KV pool
(`matches_v13_tracked`) prunes finished rows at **7 days**
(`src/match-pool.ts` `cutoff = Date.now() - 7*864e5`); `findMatchBySlug`
reads only the pool (`src/seo/data.ts`), so the page 404s. Meanwhile the
**365-day archive** (`seo_archive_v1`, `syncArchive` in `src/seo/files.ts`)
already stores 158 finished big matches (verified via the KV REST API:
Oct 9 – Nov 8, 139 KB, every record with a score) — but the serving path
never consults it. The `files.ts` comment "its page stays HTTP 200 forever"
and `llms-full.txt`'s "archive for about a year" were **aspirational, not
true**. The archive sitemap child is **empty** right now (the pool's last
finished matches aged out during the international break).

**Why this is the #1 priority:** the growth plan's whole thesis is "move
match pages from positions 10–20 into 1–10". The push-zone queries in this
export — «آتالانتا مقابل کایرات» (329 impr, pos 12.4), «موناکو مقابل
نورشلان» (244, 12.8), «منچستر یونایتد مقابل آ س رم» (210, 12.4),
«جایگاه‌های لانس درمقابل کومو» (192, 10.1), «روت‌وایس اسن مقابل آربی
لایپزیگ» (142, 13.4), «سانتاندر مقابل رئال مادرید» (62+72, ~12) — point
at pages that are **404 at the URL level**. We are ranking page-2 with dead
URLs; every week the 404 cliff deletes another ~30–40 pages of accumulated
position history. This also invalidates the M2 spelling-fix measurement
(its Leipzig/Nordsjælland/Roma pages are all past the cliff — a 404 cannot
win clicks).

**The fix (this session):** serve archived finished matches from
`seo_archive_v1` when the slug misses the pool, and list them in the
`matches-archive` sitemap child. See `docs/DECISIONS.md` (the archserve
decision) and the SEO_GSC_LOG row. **No frozen surface is touched**: live
pages render byte-identical (verified DIFF 0 after deploy); restored pages
are previously-404 URLs rendered by the same frozen templates.

**Scope limit (honest):** the archive only holds **big** matches
(`isBigForSeo` — one-big-club league fixtures + UCL/EL big pairings).
Small-market finished matches (e.g. Bologna–Monza, 79 impr / 8 clicks /
10.1% CTR) still 404 after 7 days. Quantified: ~70% of the 404 impression
mass is big-match and recoverable now; the long tail is ~1,400 impressions
across ~146 pages. Extending the archive scope is an owner decision
(proposal A/B at the end).

## 6. The pre-existing CPU cliff (found during the deploy verification — NOT caused by this session)

The post-deploy browser check surfaced Cloudflare's "Worker exceeded
resource limits" page on a fresh render of a restored page. Cloudflare
analytics (workersInvocationsAdaptive) proved the condition **pre-dates the
13.259 deploy**: **37 exceededResources errors in the 3 hours BEFORE the
deploy** (on the untouched 13.258), 13 on Nov 13, ~789 on Nov 10 (the
SEO-layer launch week). The exceeded invocations sit at exactly 10ms CPU
(the Workers free-plan limit); successful renders show P50 ≈ 1ms (cache
hits) with P99 up to ~124ms (cold renders) — i.e., **every cold match-page
render is a lottery ticket against the 10ms cliff**, and crawler bursts
(the SEO launch on Nov 10, the IndexNow ping after the archive restore,
the Nov 15 workflow smoke) buy most of the tickets. This is the same
"cold-render CPU-kill class" the v13.97-seocpu comments documented on
2026-10-14 (`GET /teams/villarreal Exceeded CPU Limit`).

**What this session did about it:** the restore made 151 immutable URLs
crawlable, so the hotfix (v13.259.1-archttl, same session) cut their
re-render cadence — ARCHIVE_PAGE_TTL 6h for archived pages (history cannot
change) + wantCached edge caching for the CPU-heavy resvg OG route (1h).
Verified live: the four previously-crashed URLs all render 200 on the new
code.

**The follow-up (a separate session, needs its own plan):** the root
render cost — a fresh match-page render parses the 139KB archive + the 346-
row pool + 7 standings tables + composes ~100KB of HTML, all near the cliff
on a cold isolate. Options worth investigating: trimming getSeoCtx for
single-match renders (the page needs one match, not the whole pool's shaped
array), moving the archive to a per-match KV shape, or the Workers Paid
plan (30s CPU) if the site grows. NOT this session's scope.
