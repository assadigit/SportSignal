# sportsignal.ir — Google Search Console audit

- **Data window:** 2026-10-10 to 2026-11-07 (the export stops at Nov 7 because Search Console lags by a few days)
- **Source:** `sportsignal_ir-Performance-on-Search-2026-11-10.zip` (Queries, Pages, Countries, Devices, Chart)
- **Method:** the `marketing-seo-audit` checklist (Search Console part) plus a read of the repo's SEO code and docs (version 13.253.0.0). Search Console data is the evidence; the repo tells us what already exists.
- **Important limit:** the site is about 4 weeks old. Treat everything below as early signals, not proof. Numbers will move a lot.

---

## 1. Plain-language summary

1. **The technical SEO is already done and is not the problem.** Server-rendered pages, canonicals, sitemap, SportsEvent markup, robots, redirects, IndexNow are all in the repo and documented. Google found and shows the site (6,945 impressions in 4 weeks).
2. **The problem is "page 2".** 65% of the search impressions we can see sit at positions 10–20, and they produced only 5 clicks. Positions 1–10 are where clicks happen.
3. **Clicks come from a handful of big games.** Four pages (PSG–Barcelona, Barcelona–Man City, Real Madrid–Leipzig, Barcelona–Aston Villa) gave 97 of 191 clicks (51%). Everything else is thin.
4. **People spell some teams differently from the site.** Searchers write «لایپزیگ», the site writes «لایپزیش»; searchers write «نورشلان», the site writes «نوردشیلند». This is cheap to fix.
5. **The best-performing pages are the "today / tomorrow / this weekend" hubs** (8.1% click rate at around position 6). Those are worth protecting and copying.
6. **Several things people search for are not on the site yet**: where to watch, lineups, timeline. Only "where to watch" converts today (3% click rate).

## 3. Findings

Format: **Issue · Impact · Evidence · Fix · Priority**.

### F1. Most impressions are on page 2
- **Impact:** High
- **Evidence:** 2,057 of 3,168 visible impressions at positions 11–20 → 5 clicks. Fifteen match pages have 100+ impressions each (3,991 impressions, 114 clicks in total); ten of them sit at position 9 or worse and together got only 33 clicks from 2,391 impressions (1.4%). Examples: Atalanta–Kairat (351 impressions, pos 12.1), Man Utd–Roma (282, pos 11.1), Monaco–Nordsjaelland (275, pos 12.5), Lens–Como (225, pos 10.6).
- **Fix:** Move these pages up with the smallest changes that close the gap: matching names (F3), more internal links to upcoming games (F7), and richer page content (F6). One change at a time (see the plan's measurement rules).
- **Priority:** 1

### F2. Click concentration on a few Barcelona / Real Madrid games
- **Impact:** Medium (risk and opportunity)
- **Evidence:** PSG–Barcelona 35 clicks, Barcelona–Man City (Dec 8) 26, Real Madrid–Leipzig 19, Barcelona–Aston Villa 17 → 97 of 191 clicks. Barcelona–Man City ranks at position 3.6 with 325 impressions **more than three weeks before kickoff**, so pages for big future games earn impressions early.
- **Fix:** Make sure upcoming big games (next 14–30 days) are linked from the homepage, hubs and both team pages from the day they exist. Check the Nov 24–25 and Dec 8–9 Champions League pages first.
- **Priority:** 2

### F3. Team names are spelled differently from how people search
- **Impact:** High (cheap fix)
- **Evidence:** site name (from `src/fa-dict.ts`) vs searcher spelling in Queries.csv:
  - RB Leipzig: site «لایپزیش» · searchers «لایپزیگ» (337 impressions across queries, e.g. «روت‌وایس اسن مقابل آربی لایپزیگ» 142, «رئال مادرید مقابل آربی لایپزیگ» 100)
  - Nordsjælland: site «نوردشیلند» · searchers «نورشلان» (275 impressions, «موناکو مقابل نورشلان»)
  - AS Roma: site «رم» · searchers also write «آ س رم» (210 impressions, «منچستر یونایتد مقابل آ س رم»)
- **Fix:** On the SEO surface only (titles, H1, descriptions, page text, JSON-LD `alternateName`), use the spelling people type, and show the other spelling once in the page text. Do not change the Telegram bot, cards or digests (they share the same name function, so keep the change local to `src/seo/`).
- **Priority:** 1

### F4. Titles use a dash where people type «مقابل»
- **Impact:** Medium, **but do not change yet**
- **Evidence:** the word «مقابل» is in the top query words (1,469 impressions plus «درمقابل» 1,209). Match titles are built as `⚽ X – Y | competition، date | اسپرت سیگنال` (`src/seo/pages.ts`).
- **Why wait:** the title template was changed on Nov 6 (leading ⚽ and the Farsi brand name, version v13.228). Only 2 days of that change are in this export. Changing titles again now would make both changes impossible to judge.
- **Fix:** run a controlled test after the Nov 6 baseline is read (about Dec 4): half the match pages get «X مقابل Y», the other half stay as they are.
- **Priority:** 3 (scheduled, not now)

### F5. Team pages show up but are not clicked
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Evidence:** 73 team pages, 833 impressions, 5 clicks (0.60%). The biggest: Heart of Midlothian (126 impressions, 0 clicks, pos 9.6), Inter Club d'Escaldes (75, 0, pos 9.0), Real Madrid (70, 1, pos 7.8).
- **Likely reason (a guess, not proven):** people searching a bare club name usually get Google's own live sports box first, so the organic result gets few clicks wherever it ranks. The current title («… — برنامه بازی بعدی و ساعت شروع») may also not read like what they want.
- **Fix:** do not spend effort here yet. After the Dec 4 read, test one title change on a handful of team pages.
- **Priority:** 4

### F6. Match pages answer "when", but people also search for "where", "lineups", "timeline"
- **Impact:** Medium-High (product decision)
- **Evidence:** "where to watch" is the only pattern with a real click rate (3.0%; one query, Bologna–Monza, got 8 clicks from 54 impressions at position 7.9, a 14.8% click rate). The lineup/positions pattern has 655 impressions and no clicks; timeline 116 and none. Match pages today carry time, venue, status, recent form, related matches. The repo has no broadcaster or lineup information.
- **Fix:** do not invent anything. First find out whether the data providers already in the repo can supply broadcaster or lineup data (report only). If they can, add it as a visible, clearly labelled block. If not, leave it out.
- **Priority:** 3

### F7. Internal links decide which upcoming games get page-1 treatment
- **Impact:** High (usually the biggest single lever)
- **Evidence:** 184 of 225 match pages have under 20 impressions each; a few get hundreds. The marketing-seo-audit playbook says internal links to pages in positions 8–20 are the most neglected lever. The repo's own docs say depth-3 crawl and sitemap-orphan checks exist; they do not measure how many links point at each *upcoming* match.
- **Fix:** every match in the next 14 days should have at least 3 internal links pointing at it (home, a hub, both team pages, sibling-fixtures card). Add a count to the SEO check so this stays true.
- **Priority:** 2

### F8. The hubs work — protect and copy them
- **Impact:** Medium-High
- **Evidence:** today / tomorrow / this-weekend: 123 impressions, 10 clicks, 8.1% click rate, average position about 6. These pages have a clear promise («بازی‌های فوتبال امروز») that matches what people type.
- **Fix:** keep them stable (no template changes). Consider competition-specific day or week hubs later, only after the Dec 4 read.
- **Priority:** 3

### F9. Rich results and structured data
- **Impact:** Low (informational)
- **Evidence:** "Search appearance" is empty. The repo already outputs SportsEvent, BreadcrumbList, SportsTeam, ItemList, Organization, WebSite markup, and the repo docs say Google shows fixtures through its own sports widget rather than a rich result. An empty row is therefore normal here, not a defect.
- **Not verified:** I could not open the live site from my tools, so I did not run Google's Rich Results Test, and Core Web Vitals field data is almost empty (one real mobile visit, LCP 1.96 s, is not a reliable number). Your repo docs list both as owner follow-ups.
- **Priority:** 4

### F10. Audience facts
- **Impact:** Low
- **Evidence:** 97% mobile, 91% Iran. Afghanistan: 393 impressions, 1 click, position 11.5 (0.25%). Afghanistan time is one hour ahead of Tehran.
- **Fix:** design every change for mobile first. A "Kabul time" line is a small idea for later, not a priority.
- **Priority:** 5

### F11. Change hygiene: too many things changed in the first month
- **Impact:** Medium
- **Evidence:** the repo log shows SEO changes on Oct 11 (full SEO layer), Nov 4 (AI bots unblocked), Nov 6 (Farsi brand + ⚽ in titles; team-title shortening for one club). The click rate fell from 3.34% to 1.93% in Nov 1–7, but impressions per day more than doubled with many pages at positions 10–14, which alone pulls the average click rate down. We cannot blame or credit any single change yet.
- **Fix:** adopt the plan's measurement rules (one change per group of pages, 3–4 week wait, a change log).
- **Priority:** 2
